
# [A] Jump
Your basic jump, it isn't much on its own, but it lets you do a lot of things
* [A] Lunge (faster depending on how fast you're falling)
* [B] Air Slap
* [Hold Z + B] Slap Pound
  * [A when landing] Spinjump (higher depending on how fast you're falling)

# [B] Slap
Your attack and miscellaneous mobility move, it can defeat foes, but also can move you up walls, and you can dance!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
* [A] Air Slap
  * [wall] Wall Slap (your "walljump", you can slap the same wall, but you'll only gain height if you slap walls facing each other)
* [Hold Z+B] Kazotsky Kick
 
# [Z] Tornado
Your mobility move, Mischa is pretty good at stirring coffee, that skill directly translates to spinning! The tornado won't end as long as you're moving your joystick in circles. You can also grab up to **5** Small enemies (goomba, bob-omb, moneybag, etc, not bullies cuz he's red and moves quickly) and regular grabbables while in the tornado
* [Spin joystick in circles] Prolonge Tornado duration
* [A] Spinjump
  * [B] Air Slap
  * [Hold Z + B] Slap Pound
  * [Approach Small enemies/Grabbables] Grab
